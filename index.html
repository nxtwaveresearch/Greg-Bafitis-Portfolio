About

Production is home base.

I started in live corporate events, producing conferences and trade shows, and moved into video from there: soundstage work at Renegade, then four years of weekly programming at Stansberry Research where I came up from audio engineer to senior producer. Since then I've run location shoots for Centurion Publishing and produced long-form film end to end at NXT Wave Research.

The work I like best is the build: launching a new show, standing up a set, working out a process that doesn't exist yet, then handing it off once it runs on its own.
