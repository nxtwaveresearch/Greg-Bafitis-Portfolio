About

From behind the board to running the set.

My career started as an audio engineer for live corporate events, and grew into a decade of producing and operating media at every level: three weekly shows for one of the largest financial publishers in the country, editors and on-location shoots, and the long-form promotional videos that drive subscription businesses.

Most recently I co-launched an independent financial publishing company and ran everything the business needed to operate: the CRM and member platforms, a seven-product subscription funnel, affiliate partnerships, video production from pre-pro through post, marketing emails and customer service. We grew from 5,000 to 12,000 subscribers in under a year with a team of two.

I'm at my best in the build phase. Launching a new show, standing up a platform, figuring out a workflow that doesn't exist yet. I like building something until it runs on its own, then handing it off.

Production will always be home base. There's nothing like running a set, and the instincts are the same ones that make a good operator: preparation, logistics, staying calm when things break.
